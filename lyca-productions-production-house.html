Subaskaran is a UK telecom billionaire who uses film production as a passion project with no budget ceiling. 2.0 (₹550 Cr budget) and Ponniyin Selvan (₹400+ Cr combined) are films no other Indian studio would fund. Lyca's financial patience is unique — willing to back ₹500 Cr projects where the break-even is 3+ years away, which is why Mani Ratnam and SS Rajamouli keep coming back.
