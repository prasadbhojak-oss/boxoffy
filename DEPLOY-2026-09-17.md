# Deployment — 17 Sep 2026

Carries the 12 Sep Google-404 package, plus a films.json merge that had to be settled first.

---

## Why films.json came first

`src/data/films.json` (19 Jun, 1,117 rows) is what `generate-year-pages.cjs` reads. Next to it sat
`films.json.bak` dated **7 Sep — newer than the file it backs up**, 1,103 rows.

Diffed both, all 37 years:

> Across **1,012 pre-2026 rows the only difference is `pageUrl`, added on 60 rows. Zero value changes.**

So `.bak` is not a backup. It is the live file with the pageUrl backfill applied, plus a rewritten 2026 —
and it was never promoted. The backfill takes rows-without-pageUrl from **66 → 0**, which clears the
standing `W4` warning outright.

`.bak` is therefore the better base everywhere except 2026, where its rewrite introduced one real defect.

---

## The defect: Drishyam 3

`.bak` 2026 carried a single row —

```
Drishyam 3 | Hindi/Malayalam | Jun 2026 | Ended | ₹110 Cr | ₹239 Cr WW | Super Hit
```

Two different films collapsed into one. The `Hindi/Malayalam` language tag is the fingerprint.

- The figures belong to **Drishyam 3 (Malayalam)** — Mohanlal, Jeethu Joseph, released 21 May 2026.
  Sacnilk lifetime: **₹110.49 Cr India net, ₹239.83 Cr WW, ₹111.70 Cr overseas, Blockbuster.**
- The **Hindi** film — *Drishyam: The Conclusion*, Ajay Devgn, **opens 2 Oct 2026** — had no row left at all.

Deploying that would have published a year-archive page asserting Drishyam 3 finished at ₹110 Cr,
two weeks before the Hindi film opens, while we publish a ₹315 Cr pre-release call on the same title.
Split back into two rows.

## Two things left open

**1 · 85 colliding `pageUrl` slugs.** Pre-existing, not introduced here — the 19 Jun file has 84 of the
same. The backfill generates slugs from titles, so same-titled films across years and languages land on
one page: `srimanthudu-box-office.html` ×3, `the-jungle-book-box-office.html` ×3,
`magadheera-box-office.html` ×2. Not a 404, so `check-links` passes it — but two films point at one page.
Needs a disambiguation rule (year or language suffix) and a separate pass.

**2 · Four backup folders.** `boxoffy - Copy`, `- Copy (2)`, `- Copy (3)` sit beside the repo, and the
`.bak` situation above came from the same habit. That is the silent-copy trap that lost the 27 year pages
and two articles in the first place. Worth deleting once this lands.
