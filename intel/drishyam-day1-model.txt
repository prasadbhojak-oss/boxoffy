HOW WE GET TO Rs 67 Cr

Rs 40.50 Cr net was banked by 5.40pm.

From our own seat count, the shows that
had already begun by then held 64% of
every advance ticket sold for the day.

But walk-ins arrive later. The 1pm to
4pm block was 47% booked and ran 69%
full. The morning was 45% booked and
ran 46%. The later a show starts, the
more it gains at the counter.

So the shows already done hold less
than 64% of the day's money. Call it
58 to 62%.

Rs 40.50 Cr / 0.62 = Rs 65 Cr
Rs 40.50 Cr / 0.58 = Rs 70 Cr

Our call: Rs 67 Cr. Band Rs 65-72 Cr.

The top is wider because night
occupancy has not been reported yet,
and night is where a word-of-mouth day
pays out.
