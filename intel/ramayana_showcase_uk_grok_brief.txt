*** LANGUAGE IS NOT A SIDE COLUMN ON THIS JOB ***
Write the language the page NAMES, in its own words: Hindi, Tamil, Telugu,
English, "Hindi with subtitles", or whatever is printed. If the page shows
no language at all, write exactly "not stated".

Here is why. In the last two days a language we were not tracking turned up
in three separate markets. Germany is playing Tamil at 17 cinemas. The UK's
Cineworld file showed 49 Telugu showtimes selling at 4.45% against Hindi's
2.16% - more than double, off a far smaller footprint. The United States
has 226 Telugu showtimes. We had been reporting all three markets as if
they were Hindi-only. Showcase is the one UK chain we have never had a
language column for, and its sites sit in exactly the towns where that
would show up.

Do not infer the language from the format badge, from 3D, or from the other
showtimes at that cinema. Only what is printed.

THE HOUSE-BLOCK RULE
If the same count appears on every showtime in the same screen and never
moves, that is seats the cinema is holding back, not sales. Put it in held,
not sold. A room reading 4 on all four of its sittings has sold nothing.
Our own test needs THREE or more sittings in a room before it can tell a
block from a coincidence, so where a screen has four or more showtimes on
the same date you are giving us something genuinely useful - say so in
notes if you spot a repeated figure.

SIX ROWS NEED A SECOND READ
Our totalling script refuses to call a market validated until six showtimes
have been read TWICE, independently. That guard exists because an Australian
figure was once wrong by 3.4x and thirteen hand reads were the only thing
that caught it.

So: when you have finished, pick the six busiest rooms you read, spread
across different cinemas, and open them again FRESH without looking at your
first figure. Send both readings.

  *** IF THE TWO DISAGREE, SAY SO AND SEND BOTH. A disagreement is the
      finding. Reconciling it quietly is the one outcome that makes the
      second read pointless. ***

Germany's last delivery promised six second reads and sent none, and the
German figure is still stamped UNVALIDATED because of it. Please do not
leave this one in the same state.

RULES - THESE MATTER MORE THAN SPEED
- Read only. Never place an order, never go past seat selection, never
  enter any personal or payment detail, never sign in, never create an
  account.
- Never estimate. If a seat map will not open, write "map would not open"
  in notes and leave the counts blank. A blank row is fine. A guessed row
  is not, because later we cannot tell it apart from a real one.
- If a showtime is listed but not bookable, write "not bookable yet" -
  that is a real finding, not a failure. Germany taught us that a listed
  showtime is not proof a show is on sale.
- If the film is not listed at a cinema at all, do not create a row for it.
  Send a short list of Showcase sites you checked that had NOTHING, so we
  can publish a denominator rather than a guess.
- Partial is fine. Work busiest-first, send what you have, keep going.

ONE CHAIN IS OFF LIMITS AND STAYS OFF
Do not open Odeon, UCI, Cinesa or any other AMC-owned site, anywhere, for
any reason. We never fetch an AMC host. Vue is also closed: it showed a bot
challenge and a bot challenge retires a host permanently - it is never
solved, worked around or retried. Neither belongs in this file.

HOW TO SEND IT BACK
The filled CSV, same columns, same order. If you can also send a
one-line-per-cinema summary with showtimes read, capacity, sold and held,
that lets us reconcile your file against its own totals before anything is
published.

AND ONE THING NOT TO SEND
Please do not re-send a file we already have in a new shape. We have had
three deliveries that turned out to be the same read re-cut, and each one
costs an hour proving it is not new. If part of a job is already done, just
say so.
