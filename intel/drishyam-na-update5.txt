North America Update 5

14,504 tickets sold. Up 38% in four
and a half hours. America woke up to
the reviews.

That is about $290,000 on the board
at a $20 average ticket, and the
first US show is still half a day
away.

Occupancy 12.2% to 16.3%.

And the schedule is moving the other
way now. 36 new showtimes appeared
at 30 cinemas since our last read.
No new sites. Just more shows inside
the ones already playing. Nobody
adds shows to a film they think is
dying.

Two shows are gone completely. AMC
Willowbrook 6pm and AMC South
Barrington 9.30pm.
