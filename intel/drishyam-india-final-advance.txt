WHY 61

Our chain seat model needs one
input, the final advance. At
4,60,000 seats it reads Rs 60-62 Cr
net on day one. Nothing is being
stretched to get there.

The multiple comes down from 10 to
9.5 times. Big openings always get
smaller multiples. Pathaan opened
Rs 55 Cr and did 9.9 times. Animal
Rs 64 Cr, 8.7 times. Jawan Rs 75 Cr,
8.5 times. The lifetime still goes
up, because the opening went up
more than the multiple came down.
