======================================================================
  HOW TO USE THIS, AND HOW NOT TO
======================================================================
  USE IT FOR: screen count, show count, city and state spread, the
  format mix, real ticket prices, and the share of shows already
  carrying a sold-out seat class. These are measurements.

  DO NOT turn any of it into seats or rupees. There is no capacity
  figure on the page, so 'sold out' tells you a class is gone, not
  how many seats it held. A 40-seat recliner class and a 900-seat
  single screen both read SOLD OUT.

  THE ONE COMPARISON WORTH PUBLISHING: set this show count against
  District's. District is the seat and gross source; BMS reaches the
  single screens District does not list. The gap between the two is
  itself the finding for a Tamil release, and it is the honest way to
  say that a District-only number under-reads Tamil Nadu.
