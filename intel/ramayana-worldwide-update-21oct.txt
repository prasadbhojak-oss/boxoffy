FIVE THINGS TO CHECK BEFORE POSTING

1. INDIA IS ONE DATE. 46,539 is 5 November only, on District alone. It is
   40% of the worldwide total off a single day, and the honest version of
   that sentence is "India is 40% and we have barely started reading it",
   not "India is 40%". If a reader divides 46,539 by nine dates they will
   get a wrong Indian daily average.

2. AUSTRALIA'S DATE SPLIT IS 84 SHORT. AU.byDate sums to 4,922 against a
   published 5,006 - it is the pre-Event-re-read split and was never
   refreshed. The 84 sits in "undated" rather than being spread across
   four days to make the row add up. Worth fixing on the page.

3. 4 NOVEMBER IS NORTH AMERICA ONLY. US previews plus Cineplex. If the day
   column is read as a worldwide curve, 4 Nov looks like a global opening
   and it is not.

4. THREE BASES IN ONE TABLE. US and Canada-Cineplex are counted seats.
   The UK, Germany, Netherlands and Landmark are seats-gone, blocks
   included. India is one platform of several. Showcase is modelled.
   The post says so; do not drop that line to save characters.

5. THE DOLLAR FIGURE IS MOSTLY OUR PRICE, NOT THEIRS. Germany is the only
   market where the cinemas print a price (and only for 49% of its
   tickets). India's rupee figure is read off District. Everything else
   applies our own assumed ATP, so $1,582,627 is a valuation, not box
   office. The page's Method panel carries this; the post should not imply
   money has changed hands.
