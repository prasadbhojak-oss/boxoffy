IF SOMEONE ASKS "WHY IS KARNATAKA ONLY 20%?"

Because Karnataka is a different market for this film, not a weak one.
It is 6.9% of the seats but 23.6% of the money — Bengaluru multiplexes
price a Rajinikanth opening at ₹754 a ticket. Low occupancy on expensive
seats is a different thing from low demand.

IF SOMEONE ASKS ABOUT MADURAI

District lists only ONE cinema in Madurai and one in Tiruchirappalli.
That is a coverage limit, not a reading about those cities. Say so
plainly rather than defending the number — the article does.
