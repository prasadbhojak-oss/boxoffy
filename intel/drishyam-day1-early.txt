Day one · early estimate · 7.15pm

Rs 40.50 Cr net was already on the
counter by 5.40pm. The night shows had
not even started.

OCCUPANCY THROUGH THE DAY

Morning 46%
Afternoon 69%
Evening 53%
Night still counting

The afternoon beat both the morning
and the evening. In advance that same
1pm to 4pm block was only 47% booked.
Everything above that walked in off
the street.

HOW WE GET TO THE NUMBER

Shows that had begun by 5.40pm held
64% of all the advance seats sold.
Walk-ins arrive later in the day, so
Rs 40.50 Cr is roughly 58 to 62% of
the full day.
