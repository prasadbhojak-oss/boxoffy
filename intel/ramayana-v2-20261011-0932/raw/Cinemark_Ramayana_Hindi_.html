Ramayana (Hindi with English Subtitles)

Add to Watch List
PG-13
3 hr 15 min
Add to Watch List

November 5, 2026

Foreign, Action, Epic, Adventure

Based on the celebrated ancient epic known to billions, Namit Malhotra’s Ramayana tells the story of Rama, a prince bound by sacrifice; Ravana, an immortal king driven by vengeance; and the prophecy that binds their fates together. It stars Ranbir Kapoor, Yash and Sam Pallavi. The film is produced by acclaimed filmmaker Namit Malhotra, whose Academy Award- wining visual effect company DNEG is behind films including The Odyssey, Dune, Interstellar, and Inception; directed by Nitesh Tiwari, whose film Dangal remains the highest-grossing Indian film worldwide; and scored by Academy Award winner Han Zimmer and A.R. Rahman. Namit Malhotra’s Ramayana will be released globally in theaters on Diwali on November 6.
