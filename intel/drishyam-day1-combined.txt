Day one · early estimate · 2 Oct

Rs 40.50 Cr net was already banked by
5.40pm. The night shows had not even
started.

Morning 46% full
Afternoon 69% full
Evening 53% full
Night still counting

The afternoon beat both the morning and
the evening. That same 1pm to 4pm block
was only 47% booked in advance.
Everything above it walked in.

HOW WE GET TO THE NUMBER

Shows begun by 5.40pm held 64% of every
advance ticket sold. Walk-ins arrive
later, so Rs 40.50 Cr is 58 to 62% of
the full day.

Rs 40.50 / 0.62 = Rs 65 Cr
Rs 40.50 / 0.58 = Rs 70 Cr
