5. WHY WE CANNOT PUBLISH AN OPENING-DAY NUMBER YET
================================================================

Our model is calibrated on one point, our own Drishyam read:
636,693 advance seats and Rs 26.97 Cr chain advance gross turning
into Rs 62.00 Cr day-one net. That is 2.30x on advance gross, or
Rs 974 per advance seat.

Run on tonight's Jailer 2 data:

  Method A   advance gross x 2.30      Rs 20.64 Cr
  Method B   seats x Rs 974            Rs 40.53 Cr

A 49% disagreement. That gap is not an error, it is the ticket price:

  Jailer 2 average ticket        Rs 216      (Tamil Nadu: Rs 179)
  rest of India                  Rs 301
  Drishyam chain advance         Rs 424-640

Tamil Nadu caps ticket prices. The same bum on the same seat yields
roughly 40% of what it yielded for Drishyam. So Method A, which is
built on money, reads far too low, and Method B, built on seats, is
the better floor.

⚠️ AND BOTH ARE STILL FLOORS, BECAUSE THE STAGES DO NOT MATCH

This is the same trap that caught us on the VenkyBO presale post, so
it gets stated plainly. The Drishyam calibration is a T-MINUS-NINE-
HOURS read. Tonight's Jailer 2 read is T-minus-two-days. Those are
not the same moment in a booking cycle, and the difference is huge:
Drishyam added 29% of its tickets in its final five hours alone,
going from 493,682 to 636,693.

So "Jailer 2 has 416,162 against Drishyam's 636,693" is NOT a
like-for-like comparison and must not be published as one. Jailer 2
has around 39 hours of booking still ahead of it that Drishyam had
already banked.

WHAT THIS MEANS IN PRACTICE: we need a second District read, ideally
T-12h or later, before any opening-day figure goes out. One read is a
level; two reads give the rate, and the rate is what forecasts a day.
Our own sweeps README already says this. Follow it.

6. WHAT BOOKMYSHOW ADDS, AND WHAT IT CANNOT
================================================================

First, the limit. BMS does not publish seat counts outside the
booking funnel. Verified on the live page: every seat field is a
label, not a number — SOLD OUT, ALMOST FULL, FILLING FAST,
AVAILABLE. There is no capacity figure. So BMS can never give us
seats sold or an advance gross, and no rupee figure should ever be
sourced to it.

What it gives us is the footprint District misses. Probe comparison,
same film, same date, same market pages:

  CHENNAI       District  690 shows / 50 cinemas
                BMS       760 shows / 58 venues      +10% shows

  COIMBATORE    District  113 shows / 10 cinemas
                BMS       198 shows / 12 venues      +75% shows

  HYDERABAD     District  123 shows / 27 cinemas
                BMS        54 shows /  9 venues      District ahead
                (BMS figure is the Tamil code only; the Telugu code
                 ET00518675 was not in this probe)

Read that carefully, because it cuts against what I expected. In
Chennai the two sources are close. In Coimbatore BMS sees nearly
twice the market. In Hyderabad District sees more. Neither source is
a superset of the other, so the real national footprint is the UNION
of the two and is larger than either number alone.

The practical consequence: our Rs 8.98 Cr advance gross is District's
coverage only. The true national advance is higher by whatever the
BMS-only cinemas hold, and in tier-2 Tamil Nadu that looks
substantial. 8.98 is a floor, and it should be labelled as one.

BMS also independently confirms the heat. Chennai returned 1,447 seat
classes across 760 shows on one date, and the SOLD OUT label is
everywhere in the early and prime bands.

ONE BMS FIELD WE DO NOT UNDERSTAND YET

BMS publishes a show-level integer, availStatus. Chennai read
0: 358 shows, 1: 184, 2: 118, 3: 100. We do NOT know the mapping.
358 sold-out shows in one city would exceed the national housefull
count being reported that day, so 0 is almost certainly not "sold
out". Nothing derived from these integers goes out until we settle
it. Use the word labels.
