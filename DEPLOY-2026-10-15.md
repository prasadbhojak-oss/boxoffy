## Gates

**Do not push if `check-links.cjs` prints anything but PASSED.**

Every `findstr` in steps 4 and 5 must print a line. If a step-4 call prints nothing, Vite served a
cached build — delete `dist\` and `node_modules\.vite\` and rebuild. If a step-5 call prints
nothing, `copy-public.cjs` did not run.

Step 2 is what actually matters for search. `generate-sitemap.cjs` enumerates `public\*.html` and
cross-references `editorials.json`, so this page is classified as an editorial at priority **0.95**,
`changefreq daily`, **and it gets a `<news:news>` tag** because the generator adds one for anything
under three days old. That news tag is the Google News entry point — it disappears if you deploy on
or after 18 October. My hand-edit to `sitemap.xml` has none of that; let step 2 overwrite it.
