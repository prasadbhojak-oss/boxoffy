Analysis Boxoffy Intelligence

Boxoffy · Box Office Intelligence · Updated May 2, 2026

Three generations of Nadiadwalas have produced films — Sajid is the modern era (started 1992 with Zulm Ki Hukumat). Nadiadwala Grandson Entertainment is the only Hindi major studio with an entire-slate exclusive Amazon Prime Video output deal for post-theatrical streaming. Sikandar (Eid 2026) underperformed significantly — Salman Khan's Eid release brand is no longer guaranteed. Pivot to Baaghi 4 (Tiger Shroff, Sep 5, 2026, A.Harsha directing) and Heropanti 3 in development. The Tiger Shroff franchises (Baaghi, Heropanti) are the studio's commercial floor — they do not break records but they break even reliably with Amazon's pre-buy backstop.

Boxoffy Note
The Sikandar disappointment is the most consequential signal for the Nadiadwala slate. If Salman's Eid releases no longer reliably cross ₹200 Cr nett, the entire Hindi star-driven Eid release model breaks down.
