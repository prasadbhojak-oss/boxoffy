Boxoffy Intelligence · The 2026-27 Slate Map
The Three Studios That Will Define the Next 12 Months

Jio + B62 (D2 success continuation): Sets the bar at ₹1,801 Cr. Maatrubhumi (Aug 14) is the first test of whether Jio can repeat without B62 directing. Toxic (Yash, Jun 4) tests Yash post-KGF. The franchise of franchises.

Red Chillies + Marflix (King): Dec 24, 2026 is the year's defining single release. SRK + Suhana on screen. Anand directing. ₹300+ Cr budget. King's success or failure cements the SRK era thesis.

Hombale + Sun Pictures: Salaar 2 (Nov 14) and Coolie (Aug 14) + Jana Nayagan (Diwali) make South cinema's H2 arguably bigger than Hindi's. Three pan-India tentpoles in 90 days, all with massive budgets.

Boxoffy Intelligence · Mid-Tier Squeeze
The ₹50-150 Cr Budget Gap Is Becoming Unviable

April 2026's data is brutal: Hindi cinema delivered a 2.2% success rate. Films with budgets between ₹50 Cr and ₹150 Cr are dying — too expensive to recoup from streaming-only deals, too small to compete for theatrical screens against ₹300 Cr+ tentpoles. Ginny Wedss Sunny 2 (₹1.57 Cr in 5 days) and Ek Din are the latest casualties.

The studios surviving this squeeze fall into three buckets: (1) Mega-budget producers (Jio, Lyca, Hombale); (2) franchise-led producers (Maddock, Marflix, B62); (3) low-budget actor-producers (Sivakarthikeyan's Passion, Cape of Good Films). The ₹100 Cr budget producer with no franchise has no obvious path forward.

Boxoffy Intelligence · The Capital Wave
₹2,500+ Cr Has Entered Indian Studios in 18 Months

The capital flows are unprecedented for Indian cinema:

· Adar Poonawalla → Dharma: ₹1,000 Cr for 50% (Oct 2024)
· Universal Music → Excel: ₹720 Cr for 30% (Jan 2026)
· Saregama → Bhansali: ₹325 Cr for 28% (Dec 2025)
· Jio Studios → Sikhya acquisition: ₹150 Cr (Feb 2026)
· Roy Kapur ongoing $50M raise: ~₹180 Cr (~$22M committed)

Total: ~₹2,500 Cr across major Hindi production houses in 18 months. Largest single wave of strategic capital ever to enter Indian cinema. The transition from owner-operator studios to professionally-capitalized creative companies has begun.
