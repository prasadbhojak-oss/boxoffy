★ Methodology · Boxoffy Streaming Earnings Estimate
Step 1 · Spotify base. Lifetime Spotify streams × $0.004 average per-stream payout = baseline USD earnings.
Step 2 · Cross-platform multiplier. Spotify captures roughly 30-40% of total Hindi music streaming revenue. Apply BMCM-weighted multiplier: multiplier = 1 + (BMCM/5).
Step 3 · Currency conversion. USD → ₹ at $1 = ₹83.5 (May 2026 baseline).

What this represents: Total rights-holder streaming revenue across Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, Amazon Music, JioSaavn. NOT artist take-home — that depends on label deals and splits between composer/lyricist/singer/publisher. For label-signed film OSTs, artist receives 10-20% of this number. For indie artists releasing through aggregators, 70-90%. YouTube views (separate from YT Music streams), sync/licensing fees, and live performance revenue are excluded.
⚠ Honest Caveat
These are estimates, not audited figures. Spotify's quoted "$0.003-0.005 per stream" is a US-weighted average — India streams pay significantly less per unit due to lower ad rates and bundled Jio/Airtel subscriptions. A song's "real" Indian streaming earnings will tend toward the lower end of our estimates. Treat these numbers as order-of-magnitude, not to-the-rupee accurate.

Boxoffy Read · April 2026
Three things this chart tells you:

1. Shashwat Sachdev is the moment. Four songs in the top 10 simultaneously from a single film's OST is extraordinary. Gehra Hua (#3) has been charting for 122 days. The D2 album has staying power that outlasts most Bollywood OSTs by months.

2. Hindi indie has arrived at scale. Bairan (#1), Sheesha (#4), Khat (#5), Dooron Dooron (#7), Sahiba (#8) — five of ten tracks are independent artists without a film or label behind them. This is a structural shift in how Hindi music is consumed, not a trend.

3. Sahiba is the chart's dark horse. 376M streams while sitting at #8 daily. Aditya Rikhari's song has been sustaining for nearly nine months. Daily position understates it — it lives in playlists and personal libraries at a scale the chart rank doesn't capture.
