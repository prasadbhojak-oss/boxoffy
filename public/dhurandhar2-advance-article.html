There are films that open big, and then there is Dhurandhar: The Revenge. The Ranveer Singh and Aditya Dhar spy epic did not just set records on its opening day — it obliterated the entire historical benchmark for Hindi cinema with a force that left the trade scrambling for comparisons. ₹102.55 crore on a non-holiday Thursday. ₹339.27 crore in four days. The biggest Hindi opener. Ever. By a distance.

What makes the numbers even more extraordinary is the context. Dhurandhar 1 opened at ₹28 crore in December 2025. The sequel opened at ₹102.55 crore. Same director. Same star. One year apart. That near-fourfold leap is a franchise growth story without precedent in Bollywood history.

“Dhurandhar 2 grossed well over 100 crore nett on its opening day. No other Bollywood film had ever reached anywhere near this mark — making the Ranveer Singh-starrer the first film ever to do so, by a big margin.”
Sacnilk — March 19, 2026

The preview night alone — ₹43 crore — shattered the previous all-India premiere record held by Pawan Kalyan’s They Call Him OG (₹25 crore) by nearly ₹18 crore. Day 2 (Friday) came in at ₹80.72 crore, a 21% drop from the record Thursday — a hold that, in absolute terms, no Hindi film had ever achieved on any single day before this film was even released. The film opened on 17,000+ screens across five languages, with the Hindi belt leading the charge on Eid, Gudi Padwa and Ugadi all falling on the same day.

Overseas, the film has been equally dominant. ₹64 crore from international markets in D0+D1 alone — US $2.95M, Canada $775K, UK $570K, Australia $515K. The North America opening weekend pre-sales crossed $6.4M before a single walk-in ticket was sold. For a Hindi film, these are numbers that simply did not exist before this weekend.
