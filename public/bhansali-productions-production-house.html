Analysis Boxoffy Intelligence

Boxoffy · Box Office Intelligence · Updated May 2, 2026

Bhansali Productions is the premier auteur-driven studio in Hindi cinema. Saregama Limited acquired 28% in December 2025 for ₹325 Cr — establishing ₹1,160 Cr valuation, the second-highest valuation event for any Hindi production house after Dharma's Poonawalla deal. Heeramandi (Netflix series, 2024) was Netflix India's most-watched original ever, beating Sacred Games. Love & War (January 2027) reunites Ranbir Kapoor + Alia Bhatt + Vicky Kaushal in the most anticipated romantic epic of the decade — co-produced with Dharma. The Bhansali playbook: ₹200+ Cr budgets, period or romantic settings, music-as-character, multi-year production timelines. No other Indian studio operates at his scale of artistic ambition combined with mainstream commercial reach.

Boxoffy Note
Love & War is a generational cinema event. SLB returning to romance after 8+ years (since Bajirao Mastani 2015) with Ranbir-Alia-Vicky triangle has the same anticipation level Padmaavat had pre-release.
