Boxoffy · Box Office Intelligence · Updated April 2026
Applause created India's prestige OTT drama market with Scam 1992 — still the highest-rated Indian show on IMDb at 9.6. Sameer Nair was STAR India CEO before founding Applause, which gives him unparalleled platform access. The pivot to theatrical with Imtiaz Ali's Main Vaapas Aaunga (Jun 2026) is the first time a pure-play streaming studio attempts a major theatrical release in India — the BO result will set a precedent.
Boxoffy Note
Applause is the only Indian studio with meaningful original content partnerships simultaneously active on SonyLIV, Amazon and Netflix — a three-platform presence no one else has managed.
