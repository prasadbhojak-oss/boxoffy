Analysis Boxoffy Intelligence

Boxoffy · Box Office Intelligence · Updated May 2, 2026

T-Series Films is the only studio in Indian cinema that can treat film production as a loss-leader for music rights. The world's #1 YouTube channel (300M+ subscribers) generates enough music royalty revenue that the film division's risk profile is fundamentally different from peers. The Bhool Bhulaiyaa franchise (BB2: ₹266 Cr; BB3: ₹390 Cr) and Drishyam co-productions with Ajay Devgn FFilms anchor the slate. Drishyam 3 (Oct 2, 2026, Gandhi Jayanti) is the next major release. T-Series's structural advantage: every film generates music IP that compounds in value across decades through YouTube views. No competitor has this revenue model.

Boxoffy Note
T-Series is the most unusual studio in the world's largest film industry — its film business loses money on most releases but the music division pays the bills. The film slate is essentially a content marketing engine for music rights. This is the bear case for asking why T-Series doesn't take more creative risks.
