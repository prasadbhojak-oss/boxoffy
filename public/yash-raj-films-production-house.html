Analysis Boxoffy Intelligence

Boxoffy · Box Office Intelligence · Updated May 2, 2026

Yash Raj Films remains the only major Indian studio that refuses to sell OTT rights before theatrical release, treating cinemas as the primary creative format rather than a marketing channel for streaming. The YRF Spy Universe alone has grossed ₹4,200+ Cr across Pathaan (₹1,055 Cr WW), War, Tiger 3 (₹466 Cr WW), and Tiger Zinda Hai (₹565 Cr WW) — the most lucrative franchise in Hindi cinema history. War 2 (Aug 14, 2026 Independence Day) brings Hrithik Roshan + Jr NTR + Kiara into the universe in YRF's most ambitious cross-language tentpole yet. King (Dec 24, 2026) features YRF as overseas distribution partner. The 2025 UK setup signals a long-term play to control international distribution rather than license it out.

Boxoffy Note
YRF's no-pre-OTT-sale stance is the rarest position in Indian cinema in 2026. Every other major studio has accepted Netflix, Prime, JioHotstar pre-buys to de-risk theatrical. YRF's bet: theatrical exclusivity plus longer windows preserves library value over a 30-year horizon. Spy Universe is the proof point.
