▎THE STORY IN ONE SCREEN

The Two Biggest Indian Films in History — Now ~₹31 Cr Apart

For nine years, Baahubali 2: The Conclusion was the answer to "what's the biggest Indian film ever?" — ₹1,810 Cr worldwide, ₹825 Cr India nett, an opening weekend that rewrote what Indian cinema's ceiling looked like. As of Day 40 (April 28, 2026), Dhurandhar 2: The Revenge sits at ~₹1,779 Cr worldwide per Sacnilk + Box Office India. Still running. Still adding ~₹2 Cr a week in India and another ₹50 lakh weekly internationally. The gap is now down to ~₹31 Cr — small enough that one strong weekend closes it. Some trade outlets (Koimoi, News24) have already called the record broken citing ₹1,801 Cr WW. We're staying conservative with the Sacnilk-aligned figure until the lead is decisive.

This is not a story about which film is better. Baahubali 2 had Rajamouli's two-part mythological scale, an audience that had been waiting since 2015, and the question "Why did Kattappa kill Baahubali?" carrying its 12-month marketing tail. Dhurandhar 2 had Aditya Dhar following up the highest-grossing Hindi film ever (Dhurandhar 1, ₹1,355 Cr WW), a Ranveer Singh post-Padmaavat career resurrection, and the post-pandemic trade environment of 2026.

What's interesting is what the numbers themselves say about how each film got there. Different opening days. Different market splits. Different long-tail behaviors. Different overseas performance. One head-to-head can't capture all of it, but the numbers below are the ones that matter.

▎THE FIRST FILMS

How Each Sequel Got Set Up — And the Jump That Followed

Both sequels inherited audiences from films that had already broken records of their own. But the jump from Part 1 to Part 2 in each franchise tells two completely different stories. Baahubali 2 was a cultural earthquake driven by 24 months of "Why did Kattappa kill Baahubali?" anticipation that pulled audiences from B/C/D centers who had never seen a multiplex before. Dhurandhar 2 was a linear, well-executed action-sequel uplift. The numbers below tell that story:

▎Why the BB Jump Was Bigger
Baahubali 1 finished at ₹650 Cr WW with ~5.5 Cr footfalls. Baahubali 2 nearly doubled both — ₹1,810 Cr WW with ~10 Cr footfalls. The sequel pulled in audiences who had never been to a multiplex before, in B, C, D, even E centers across India. Trade reports describe single-screens in Tier-3 and Tier-4 towns running BB2 at 100% occupancy for two straight weeks — something that hadn't happened since Sholay.

Dhurandhar 1 already did ~3.55 Cr footfalls (a post-pandemic record). D2 added another ~1.2 Cr footfalls on top — strong, but linear sequel uplift, not the cultural phenomenon BB2 delivered. The BB franchise jump was helped by two unique factors not available to D2: (1) a 21-month gap between releases vs D2's 3-month gap from D1, and (2) a U/UA universal certificate vs D2's A-rating. BB2 was a film grandparents took grandchildren to see; D2 had to be 18+.

▎The GCC Asterisk
Both Dhurandhar 1 and Dhurandhar 2 were banned across UAE-GCC countries due to political content concerns. Trade analysts (Suniel Wadhwa of Karmic Films, Atul Mohan, and Bollywood Hungama's overseas desk) estimate D2 would have earned an additional ₹65-85 Cr from Gulf markets had it released — enough to push WW past Baahubali 2's record. The "GCC factor" is the central caveat in any 2026-vs-2017 head-to-head.

▎WHY THE SHAPES ARE DIFFERENT

Two Films, Two Eras, Two Audiences

1. Ticket pricing changed everything

Average ticket price in India in 2017 was ~₹110-150. In 2026 it's ~₹290 for D2's Hindi version (BOI confirmed) — roughly double. That's why Dhurandhar 2 will finish with around half the footfalls (~4.75 Cr all-language vs Baahubali 2's ~10 Cr) but more revenue in absolute rupees. Whether D2 is a "bigger" film than BB2 in cultural terms depends entirely on whether you're measuring revenue or admits — by ticket count, BB2's run is in a different league entirely (still the only 21st-century film in India's all-time top-5 admits list, behind only Sholay, Mughal-e-Azam and Mother India).

2. Overseas markets behaved completely differently

Baahubali 2 dominated GCC and the rest of the world (₹247 + ₹487 = ₹734 Cr from those two regions alone). Dhurandhar 2's overseas total was ₹423 Cr — entirely earned without a Gulf release. North America alone delivered $30M+ (₹215 Cr), the all-time Indian record. The shape difference is primarily about diaspora composition and the GCC ban — BB2 was a 4-language pan-India film with massive Gulf and SE Asia footprint, while D2 leaned Hindi-only and the Hindi diaspora skews North American/UK-dominated.

3. The window strategy

Baahubali 2 had a clean 4-week theatrical window before any home video release. Dhurandhar 2's OTT premiere was originally scheduled for Apr 21 and got delayed to mid-May after JioHotstar saw the theatrical leg holding. That extra runway is part of why D2's late-tail income (₹1.4 Cr in W6D1) is still meaningful enough to threaten the BB2 record.

4. Competitive context

BB2 opened in a 2017 box office where the only major competitor was Begum Jaan and a couple of Hollywood holdovers. D2 opened in March 2026 against Bollywood's most crowded summer pre-window in five years — yet still finished with the bigger India nett. That speaks more to D2's draw than any structural advantage.

▎THE BOXOFFY VERDICT

Both Are All-Time Blockbusters. The Order Depends on What You Measure.

If "biggest" means India nett — Dhurandhar 2 wins. If "biggest" means worldwide gross — Baahubali 2 still holds the record at ₹1,810 Cr, but D2 has closed to within ~₹31 Cr (Sacnilk D40) and the fall is days away. If "biggest" means footfalls — Baahubali 2 wins comfortably. If "biggest" means North America impact — Dhurandhar 2 has set a new ceiling.

The honest answer is that Baahubali 2 changed what was possible for Indian cinema and Dhurandhar 2 has stretched that ceiling further in nominal terms. They're not really comparable on a like-for-like basis because everything that makes a film "big" — ticket prices, screen counts, OTT economics, overseas reach, language strategy — has shifted between 2017 and 2026.

What's settled: Baahubali 2 is still the highest-grossing Indian film worldwide per Sacnilk and BOI as of D40 (Apr 28). What's open: how soon D2 closes the remaining ~₹31 Cr — most projections say within 7-14 days. The trade press is split, with Koimoi/News24 already declaring the record broken citing ₹1,801 Cr WW; Sacnilk and Box Office India keep it intact. Boxoffy holds the conservative line until the lead is decisive.

▎FAQ

Common Questions

Has Dhurandhar 2 beaten Baahubali 2 worldwide?
Per Sacnilk and Box Office India: not yet, but the gap is now ~₹31 Cr. As of April 28, 2026 (Day 40), Dhurandhar 2 sits at approximately ₹1,779 Cr worldwide vs Baahubali 2's lifetime record of ₹1,810 Cr. Some trade outlets (Koimoi, News24) call the record broken citing D2 at ₹1,801 Cr WW — but that uses a rounded BB2 lifetime of ₹1,800 Cr; we hold to ₹1,810 Cr per Wikipedia + Sacnilk + BOI consensus. Either way the fall is imminent, likely within 7-14 days.
Has Dhurandhar 2 beaten Baahubali 2 in India?
Yes — convincingly. Dhurandhar 2's India nett of ₹1,167 Cr is ₹342 Cr higher than Baahubali 2's ₹825 Cr, making it the highest-grossing Hindi film ever in India and the second-highest in any Indian language after KGF Chapter 2's all-language nett.
Why is Baahubali 2's worldwide higher despite a smaller India number?
Baahubali 2 had a much larger overseas market — ₹985 Cr total — driven by GCC (₹247 Cr) and Rest of World (₹487 Cr) on the back of its 4-language pan-India footprint. Dhurandhar 2's overseas (₹423 Cr) is less than half of BB2's, with a key asterisk: D2 was banned in GCC entirely. Despite that handicap, D2 set the new North America record at $30M+. The overseas gap is the entire story behind the worldwide difference, and the GCC ban is the main driver of that gap.
Is Dhurandhar 2 the biggest North American Indian release ever?
Yes. Dhurandhar 2 crossed Baahubali 2's $20.78M North American lifetime in early April 2026 (around Week 4) and crossed $30M in late April, becoming the first Indian film to clear that milestone. Canada alone contributed a record-breaking $10.14M (₹85 Cr), the highest-ever for any Indian film in that country. The previous overall record (BB2's $20.78M) had stood for nine years.
Did either film recover its budget?
Both, comfortably. Baahubali 2 made ₹1,810 Cr WW against a ₹250 Cr budget — a 7.2× return. Dhurandhar 2 has made ~₹1,779 Cr WW against ₹300 Cr — a 5.93× return that will likely improve to 6× before the theatrical run ends. Both films' OTT/satellite packages added another ₹245 Cr (D2) and an estimated ₹120 Cr (BB2) on top.
