Boxoffy OTT Take · April 2026
This is as strong an OTT month as India has seen in 2026. The Boys finale is a cultural moment — Prime Video has been building to this since 2019. BEEF Season 2 is a genuine risk (entirely new cast, new story) from Netflix. If it works, it cements BEEF as an anthology IP. If it doesn't, the Emmy halo fades fast.

For Hindi originals, Maamla Legal Hai S2 is the one to watch — Season 1 trended at #3 in India and had a 7.8 IMDb. Legal comedies with authentic courtroom flavour are undersupplied in Indian OTT and Ravi Kishan is perfectly cast. Sitaare Zameen Par on SonyLIV is the theatrical film that deserves a second look — Aamir's best work in years by most accounts.

The Dhurandhar 2 OTT date is the most searched thing in Indian entertainment right now. The ₹150 Cr JioHotstar deal is confirmed. Typical 8-week window from theatrical release (Mar 19) would put premiere around mid-May. Nothing official yet — we'll update the moment it drops.
FAQ

Frequently Asked — OTT India April 2026

When does The Boys Season 5 release in India? +
The Boys Season 5 releases on April 8, 2026 on Amazon Prime Video India. This is the final season of the superhero satire starring Karl Urban, Jack Quaid, and Antony Starr. Hindi dubbed audio is available alongside the original English. The show has been Prime Video's most-watched original in India for three consecutive seasons.
When does Maamla Legal Hai Season 2 release on Netflix? +
Maamla Legal Hai Season 2 releases on April 3, 2026 on Netflix India. The courtroom comedy-satire stars Ravi Kishan and has 10 new episodes. Season 1 trended at #3 in India on Netflix. The show is set in a fictional district court and satirises India's legal system.
When does Dhurandhar: The Revenge release on JioHotstar OTT? +
Dhurandhar: The Revenge's JioHotstar OTT release date has not been officially announced. The ₹150 Crore streaming rights deal with JioHotstar is confirmed. Based on the standard 8-week theatrical window from its March 19, 2026 release, the OTT premiere is estimated around mid-May to early June 2026. Boxoffy will update this page the moment an official date is confirmed.
When does BEEF Season 2 release? Is it the same cast? +
BEEF Season 2 releases on Netflix India on April 16, 2026. No — it is NOT the same cast. BEEF is an anthology series. Season 2 has an entirely new story and new cast; it is unconnected to the Steven Yeun and Ali Wong storyline from Season 1. The Emmy-winning format returns with fresh characters.
What are the new shows on Netflix India this week? +
New shows and movies on Netflix India this week (April 3–10, 2026): Maamla Legal Hai Season 2 (April 3), Mrithyunjay — Telugu thriller (April 3), Vadh 2 — Hindi crime thriller (April 3), XO Kitty Season 3 (streaming from March 30). Coming later in April: BEEF Season 2 (April 16), Full Swing Season 4 (April 16), Ustaad Bhagat Singh — Pawan Kalyan (April 23).
Which OTT platform has the best content in India in 2026? +
It depends on what you watch. Netflix India leads in global originals (The Crown, Squid Game, BEEF) and has the most Hindi-language OTT films. Prime Video dominates with The Boys, Mirzapur, Panchayat, and Indian originals. JioHotstar has IPL live streaming, Star India content, Disney films, and holds Dhurandhar 2's streaming rights. SonyLIV is the home of Maharani, Shark Tank India, and Sitaare Zameen Par. Based on Indian-language content alone, according to Ormax Media's 2025 report, JioHotstar led with 188.7 million viewers vs Netflix's 185.3 million.
When does Sitaare Zameen Par release on OTT? +
Sitaare Zameen Par releases on SonyLIV on April 3, 2026. Aamir Khan's sports drama is a spiritual successor to Taare Zameen Par (2007). The film follows a basketball coach assigned to train a team of neurodivergent players. Darsheel Safary, who played the child protagonist in Taare Zameen Par, also appears in the film.
