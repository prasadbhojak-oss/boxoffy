Boxoffy Editorial · Week 3 · Apr 13–19, 2026
BEEF S2 Leads. South India Arrives. Euphoria Divides.
BEEF Season 2 takes the top spot on Netflix India in its debut week — and it earns it. The anthology gamble paid off. Oscar Isaac and Cailee Spaeny in a country-club power war is exactly the kind of prestige television Indian urban audiences have been starved of since the original BEEF. The move to anthology format, widely criticised before release, is now the thing people are talking about in its favour. The bigger India story this week is South cinema's OTT moment: Ustaad Bhagat Singh and Youth both landed on Netflix within 24 hours of each other. Both debuted in the top 5. Pan-India theatrical carry-over is real and the streaming numbers will confirm it. Euphoria Season 3 is this week's most-watched argument. 44% on Rotten Tomatoes is the lowest in franchise history — but it's still #1 globally on HBO Max. In India, the premium urban audience is watching and debating. That's not disappointment; that's engagement. Monday hold for Matka King and Assi will be the week's key data watch.
