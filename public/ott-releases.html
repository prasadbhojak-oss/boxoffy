EDITOR’S NOTE · Updated Thursday, July 31, 2026

Ikka Delivers Netflix a Dhurandhar-Scale Hit — and Reopens the Sunny Deol Streaming Playbook.

The takeaway from July is that Ikka, the Sunny Deol–Akshaye Khanna reunion that dropped on Netflix on July 10, has done what almost no direct-to-streaming Hindi film has managed this year: it has entered the conversation as a genuine peer to Ranveer Singh’s Dhurandhar: The Revenge, the JioHotstar/Netflix crossover that Ormax has already crowned the top Hindi streaming film of the first half at 35.2 million viewers. Three weeks in, the buzz signals around Ikka (chart position, social velocity, sustained watch-through) point to opening-window viewership tracking in the same 25–35 million band.

That is a significant win for Netflix, which has spent much of 2026 watching JioHotstar and Prime Video absorb the biggest Hindi-language moments. It also revives an old streaming truth: the reunion of two 1990s box-office titans, in the year of their Border anniversary, still moves the needle harder than any algorithmically curated release calendar. Expect the platform’s late-2026 slate to lean into that lesson.

Elsewhere this month, Ted Lasso returned on Apple TV+ after three years with a new premise (a women’s second-division club), Panchayat: Season 5 continues to hold on Prime Video as the year’s highest-completion Hindi original, and the platform pipeline reshapes for August: Welcome to the Jungle, Maharani S2, Operation Safed Sagar and Reacher S3 all lock into a theatrical-to-digital catch-up wave set to define the month.

The State of Streaming · July 2026

The Money Has Arrived — Now It Wants to Be Watched.

India's streaming business has crossed the line from land-grab to profit-hunt. The subscribers are here — 700 million-plus of them. The question every platform is now asking isn't "how do we grow?" but "how do we make this pay?"

The investment story is a Reliance story. Since folding Disney+ Hotstar into JioCinema in February 2025, JioStar has poured roughly $10 billion into content across three years — ₹33,000 crore ($3.85B) in FY26 alone — and it's working: Reliance's media & entertainment revenue nearly doubled to ₹40,682 crore in FY26, with EBITDA up 219%. JioHotstar now commands ~23% of viewer interest (tied with Prime Video), 48% of the Hindi pay-GEC market, and seven of its top ten shows. Netflix (~19%) and the regional players (ZEE5 ~10%, SonyLIV ~5%) are fighting for the rest.

But the real 2026 movement is the pivot from subscribers to monetisation. With ARPU stuck at $2–3 — a fraction of the West — pure subscription can't fund ₹33,000-crore content slates. So the playbook has changed on three fronts: ad-supported tiers are now standard; commerce-led streaming is live (JioHotstar let IPL 2026 viewers order food mid-match); and AI-led discovery arrived via a JioHotstar–OpenAI tie-up for conversational search, aimed at pushing viewers deeper than the marquee sports and tentpoles. Micro-content — MX Player's vertical "Fatafat" dramas chasing a $300M market — is the newest front.

Consolidation is the other throughline. The merger created a giant; now analysts expect the smaller and regional players to combine or exit, chasing a 15–20 million-subscriber base big enough to matter. Money is moving at the edges too — Universal Music took a stake in Excel Entertainment (₹747 cr), audio-series platform Pocket FM raised $103M, and Prime Video is pushing the other way, committing to 4–6 theatrical releases a year as streaming and cinema blur. The event title is the new currency: a marquee drop (an Aryan Khan directorial, a Ranveer Singh extended cut, a Sunny Deol comeback) now pulls brand integrations and attention the way an IPL match does.

What it means for what you're watching: more money, more regional depth (60–65% of viewing is now non-Hindi), and a hard tilt toward titles big enough to justify the spend. The charts below are the output of all that capital — ranked by the metric the platforms themselves now chase hardest: watch-time.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is the biggest OTT release in India this week?

For the week of July 24–30, 2026, Ikka (Netflix) is the marquee Hindi premiere — Sunny Deol and Akshaye Khanna's first film together since Border (1997). Ranveer Singh's Dhurandhar: The Revenge (JioHotstar) remains the biggest overall streaming draw.

What is the most-watched Indian web series of all time?

By sustained viewership and cultural footprint, Prime Video's franchises lead — The Family Man, Mirzapur and Panchayat — with SonyLIV's Scam 1992 and JioHotstar's Criminal Justice close behind. Criminal Justice Season 4 was the single most-watched original of 2025 per Ormax, with 27M+ views.

How does Boxoffy rank OTT titles without official view counts?

India has no public, standardised OTT viewership metric. Boxoffy's charts blend Ormax Media's viewership estimates (the industry's most credible source), release prominence, and search-trend buzz. Weekly and monthly rankings are editorial buzz reads; the all-time chart reflects sustained viewership plus cultural impact.

Which OTT platform is biggest in India in 2026?

JioHotstar (the merged Jio + Disney+ Hotstar) leads on total scale and sports rights, Prime Video dominates premium originals, and MX Player has the largest free/AVOD reach at roughly 250M monthly users. India's streaming market is projected at ~549M users and $4.5B in 2026.

Methodology: Watch-time is the primary ranking metric (billions of minutes, B min). India publishes no per-title watch-time or view data, so every watch-time figure on this page is a BCM estimate, not a disclosed platform number. The BCM model is: est. viewers × content runtime × completion factor — viewers anchored to Ormax Media estimates where they exist (e.g., Criminal Justice S4 ≈ 27M), completion assumed ~0.5–0.65 for series and ~0.7–0.85 for films. Weekly figures reflect the current week, monthly the July window, all-time the cumulative streaming life (incl. multiple seasons and rewatches). Figures are rounded to signal their estimated nature and will be revised as Ormax StreamView data lands.
