Why It Owns The Big Screen

The format is the movie.

The Odyssey is the first film in history shot entirely on IMAX film cameras. That single fact is why premium-format demand — the IMAX and PLF sellouts driving both the India and US numbers — isn't hype. On the biggest screens you literally see more of the movie. Here's the map of formats, to scale.

FormatRatioMediumWhy it matters for Odyssey
IMAX 70mm 15-perf film · the pinnacle 1.43:1 70mm film The whole frame. 15 perfs run horizontally through the projector; largest, highest-res image, floor-to-ceiling. Only 41 screens worldwide.
IMAX Digital dual-laser 1.90:1 Digital Taller than scope, far more available — this is what most "IMAX" tickets are, including nearly all of India's.
70mm 5-perf 2.20:1 70mm film Classic large-format film (Dunkirk-style) — gorgeous grain and depth, wider and shorter than IMAX.
Dolby Cinema Dolby Vision + Atmos 2.39:1 Digital Best-in-class sound and black levels, laser HDR, recliners — a scope frame, so you trade vertical picture for contrast and comfort.
35mm the "classic four-perf" 2.39:1 35mm film Traditional photochemical scope. Warm and filmic; the smallest of the film formats on offer here.
6.5% Share of Oppenheimer's entire US gross delivered by a single IMAX screen (Melbourne, 2023). That's the multiplier a 100%-IMAX film is built to repeat — and why these seats sell out a year out.
You see the whole film
Because every frame was shot on IMAX, the 1.43:1 presentation shows the full image edge to edge. Every wider format trims the top and bottom — so on IMAX 70mm you're watching more movie, not just a bigger one.
Scarcity is the engine
Just 41 true IMAX 70mm screens on Earth (25 in the US) means concentrated demand, premium pricing, and a wave of "see it in IMAX" word-of-mouth that fuels repeat viewings across a long run.
Immersion that rewards the trip
Nolan's pitch is that the giant square frame makes the screen "disappear" and fills your peripheral vision — a near-3D effect without glasses. New, quieter cameras finally let him shoot dialogue scenes this way too.
India runs on premium
True 1.43 film is limited to those 41 venues, so India's sellouts in Mumbai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru & Delhi are IMAX Digital and premium large formats — still the engine behind the biggest Hollywood open of 2026 here.
