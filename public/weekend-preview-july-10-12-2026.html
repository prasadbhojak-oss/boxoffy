Here's the setup for July 10–12: one big new release, and a question mark hanging over it. Dhamaal 4 is the biggest Indian opening of the fortnight — IMDb's most-anticipated Indian film of the year, a comedy franchise with a genuine track record. But it doesn't get a clean run. Akshay Kumar's Welcome to the Jungle is three weeks in and still pulling exactly the family-comedy crowd Dhamaal wants. Two comedies, one audience. That's the whole story of the weekend, and it's why our read comes with a wider band than usual.

Also this weekend · On streaming

The weekend's other big Bollywood story skips theatres entirely. Ikka drops on Netflix the same day (July 10) — a courtroom thriller that reunites Sunny Deol and Akshaye Khanna for the first time since Border (1997), nearly three decades ago. It's Sunny Deol's streaming debut, so it won't touch a box-office chart — but that Border-reunion nostalgia makes it the weekend's biggest at-home draw. Early reviews are mixed (2.5–3.5 stars); for 90s Bollywood fans, the pairing alone is the event.

03The US board: Moana rolls the dice into a family logjam

Here's the neat symmetry of the weekend — both markets are staging the same fight. In India it's Dhamaal 4 landing on a holdover comedy; in the US it's a new family tentpole landing on one that's only a week old. Disney sends its live-action Moana into American cinemas, the hardest call on the calendar. The remake floor is high (Aladdin opened to $91M, The Little Mermaid to $95M) and the animated Moana is a streaming monster, but remake fatigue is real and this spring's Snow White flop haunts the whole strategy. Our read is a deliberately wide $45–75M with low confidence.

The catch: Moana isn't landing in open water. Minions & Monsters is only a week old and owns the exact same family audience, and Young Washington — Angel Studios' surprise $20.8M debut — has the faith-and-family base that historically holds unusually well. It's a crowded kids' matinee market, and that overlap is the real cap on Moana's ceiling.

05FAQ

When does Dhamaal 4 release and how much will it open?

Dhamaal 4 releases July 10, 2026. Boxoffy's base case is a Day 1 of ₹13–19 crore nett (central ₹16 Cr) and an opening weekend of ₹52–68 crore, with a ₹120 crore lifetime read if word of mouth holds. Trade tracking (Pinkvilla ₹14–19 Cr, Koimoi ₹13–15 Cr) lines up with the base.

What's competing with Dhamaal 4 this weekend?

Its main competition is a holdover: Akshay Kumar's Welcome to the Jungle, in week 3, which draws the same family-comedy audience. Alpha (week 2) is fading fast after a 71% Monday drop, and the rest of the India chart is in its long tail. In the US, the live-action Moana opens the same weekend.

What is the Boxoffy Hype Index score for Dhamaal 4?

74 out of 100 — "Hit Watch" tier. It reflects Dhamaal 4's status as IMDb's most-anticipated Indian film of 2026 and a proven comedy franchise, held back only by a crowded family-comedy lane with Welcome to the Jungle still running.

Is Ikka releasing in theatres or on Netflix?

Ikka is a direct-to-streaming release — it premieres worldwide on Netflix on July 10, 2026, the same day Dhamaal 4 opens in cinemas. The courtroom thriller reunites Sunny Deol and Akshaye Khanna for the first time since Border (1997) and marks Sunny Deol’s streaming debut, so it does not appear on theatrical box-office charts.

What movies are in US theaters the weekend of July 10–12, 2026?

The new wide release is Disney's live-action Moana (Boxoffy read $45–75M). It faces a crowded holdover field led by Minions & Monsters (week 2, ~$18–22M), Toy Story 5 (week 4), Young Washington (week 2), Supergirl, Disclosure Day and the long-running Obsession — a family-audience logjam that mirrors India's Dhamaal 4 vs Welcome to the Jungle comedy clash.

How accurate have Boxoffy's recent predictions been?

Mixed, and we publish both. We called Welcome to the Jungle's ₹19 crore Day 1 to the rupee. We missed on Alpha's North America opening (predicted $1.3–2.6M, it did $574K) and were caught off guard by Maa Inti Bangaaram's ₹23 crore opening. The misses have been folded back into the model.
