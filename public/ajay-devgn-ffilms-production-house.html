Analysis Boxoffy Intelligence

Boxoffy · Box Office Intelligence · Updated May 2, 2026

Ajay Devgn FFilms is one of the few major Hindi production houses where the actor-producer is genuinely active in both roles. Tanhaji (2020, ₹279 Cr nett) is the only Hindi film of the pandemic era where the lead star also produced and the film cleared ₹250+ Cr. Two active franchises anchor the slate: Drishyam (with T-Series, IP-owning partner) and Golmaal (Rohit Shetty cop universe). Drishyam 3 (Oct 2, 2026, Gandhi Jayanti) is one of the year's top 5 Hindi releases. Ajay Devgn's banner is not as heavily capitalized as Dharma or Excel, but his hands-on creative + commercial role keeps the slate disciplined.

Boxoffy Note
Ajay is one of only 3 Hindi A-list stars who genuinely produce their own films (Aamir, SRK being the others). Salman dabbles via SKF but rarely takes commercial risk. Ajay's Drishyam franchise IP is one of the most valuable mid-budget franchises in Hindi cinema.
