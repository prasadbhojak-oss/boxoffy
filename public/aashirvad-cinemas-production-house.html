Analysis Boxoffy Intelligence

Boxoffy · Box Office Intelligence · Updated May 2, 2026

Aashirvad Cinemas is the architect of Kerala cinema's global ambitions. The Prithviverse (Lucifer 2019 → L2: Empuraan 2025) is Malayalam's only genuine cinematic universe. L2: Empuraan crossed ₹350+ Cr WW (2025) — the biggest Malayalam film since 2022. Antony Perumbavoor is Mohanlal's manager-turned-producer; the relationship is the central asset. Co-produced Patriot (May 1, 2026, ₹29.37 Cr WW D1) with Anto Joseph FC — the Mammootty + Mohanlal reunion after 17 years. Malayalam cinema's commercial ceiling has been raised significantly by Aashirvad's willingness to invest at scale (₹100+ Cr budgets, unprecedented for Mollywood until L2). The Drishyam franchise (Mohanlal's original Malayalam version that spawned the Hindi remake by Ajay Devgn) is core Aashirvad IP.

Boxoffy Note
Aashirvad is the only Malayalam studio that has consistently broken the regional ceiling and operated at pan-Indian commercial scale. Patriot's success now extends the partnership network into Wayfarer + Anto Joseph FC. Malayalam cinema's commercial transformation is largely an Aashirvad story.
