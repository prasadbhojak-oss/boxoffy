Cultural Impact
Dangal didn't just break box office records — it broke cultural ones. A film about women wrestlers from Haryana, made in Hindi, became the biggest Indian film in Chinese history. It tapped into a universal anxiety about daughters, ambition, and a father's belief in a society that doesn't share it. In China, it grossed more than any Hollywood blockbuster that year. That sentence remains almost impossible to explain — and that is exactly what makes Dangal singular.
Boxoffy Take
Dangal's worldwide total of ₹2,059 Cr is driven substantially by its China theatrical run of ₹1,100+ Cr — the largest overseas run ever for an Indian film. India nett stands at ₹374 Cr. Worldwide it is #1. The China performance demonstrated that Hindi cinema can find a mass audience far beyond the diaspora when the storytelling is universal. A remarkable data point that the industry is still trying to replicate.

Cultural Impact
Sultan was peak Salman Khan — a film that gave him an emotional arc matched to a physical one, and allowed his mass audience to see him as both invincible and vulnerable. The wrestling backdrop was genuine and researched; the emotional core was a marriage fractured by ego and repaired by competition. Salman's physical transformation for the role remains one of Bollywood's most discussed body-of-work moments.
Boxoffy Take
₹586 Cr worldwide in 2016 is worth approximately ₹1,100 Cr in today's ticket prices. Sultan was the film that proved YRF had cracked the Salman formula — something Ek Tha Tiger had started and Sultan completed. One of the few Salman films where critics and audiences aligned.

Cultural Impact
Sanju is the most commercially successful biopic in Indian history. Whether it is a great biopic is debated — it was accused of whitewashing. But Ranbir Kapoor's transformation into Sanjay Dutt was so complete that it became one of the talking points of the decade. The film arrived at a moment when Dutt's story — drugs, guns, jail, redemption — felt more relevant than ever.
Boxoffy Take
₹586 Cr worldwide on a story about a Bollywood actor who went to jail for illegal weapons possession. The film's first week collections were historic. A reminder that the masala biopic — rather than the reverent one — is the format that actually works commercially.

Cultural Impact
Padmaavat released under unprecedented duress — protests, vandalism, bounties on the lead actress's head. It released anyway and made ₹585 Cr worldwide. Ranveer Singh's Alauddin Khilji became the defining villain performance in modern Indian cinema — so complete and committed that the film became his as much as Deepika Padukone's. Bhansali's visual grammar reached its apex here.
Boxoffy Take
Released in a truncated format with no IMAX and limited screens due to protests — and still crossed ₹300 Cr India nett. What the film would have achieved under normal conditions is unknowable. Ranveer Singh's career-defining performance was awarded by every critics' body, yet everyone only talked about Khilji.

How We Calculate These Numbers
India NettBox Office India is the primary source. Pre-2017 = pre-GST (Entertainment Tax deducted). Post-Jul 2017 = post-GST (18% flat). Not directly comparable across eras without adjustment.
Worldwide GrossIndia Gross + Overseas Gross. Dangal's China run (₹1,100 Cr) and Secret Superstar's China run (₹750 Cr) are the two outliers — both were driven by Aamir Khan's exceptional Chinese market penetration.
Inflation AdjustmentWW Gross adjusted via RBI CPI India index (base 2026). ₹100 Cr in 2016 ≈ ₹148 Cr in 2026 purchasing power. Dangal inflation-adjusted: ₹3,047 Cr — still the all-time leader by a wide margin.
FootfallsIndia Nett (₹) ÷ Blended Average Ticket Price for the year. ATP rose from ₹88 (2010) to ₹195 (2025). Higher ATP = fewer footfalls for same nett. Baahubali 2 holds the footfall record despite lower absolute nett than Pushpa 2.
OTT ViewershipNetflix and Amazon Prime do not universally publish India-specific data. Figures marked "est." are trade estimates from Film Information and ORMAX Media. Netflix hours (Dhurandhar's 101.3M) are officially confirmed.
Budget ROIIndia Nett ÷ Production Budget — India theatrical only. Does not include OTT rights, satellite deals, music, or overseas. A 1× ratio on India theatrical alone is considered healthy given secondary revenue streams typically add 40–70% on top.

Cultural Impact
Tiger Zinda Hai gave Salman Khan's Tiger universe its definitive statement. The action was bigger, the set pieces more elaborate, and Katrina Kaif's role was substantially expanded. The Christmas release became a self-fulfilling phenomenon — audiences had come to expect Tiger Zinda Hai as a holiday tradition before it even released.
Boxoffy Take
₹565 Cr worldwide in 2017 made it the highest-grossing Bollywood film of that year. The YRF Spy Universe — Tiger, War, Pathaan — has now crossed ₹3,000 Cr combined worldwide, making it the highest-grossing franchise in Indian cinema history. Tiger Zinda Hai was the film that cemented the universe's commercial viability.
