What This Comparison Actually Tells Us

Comparing these two films directly is not straightforward — and anyone who presents a simple winner is missing the story. They are fundamentally different productions aimed at different audiences in different windows with different structural advantages.

Pushpa 2 had three things Dhurandhar 2 didn't: A Christmas Day opening (the biggest single box office day in Indian history, worth ₹168 Cr), a full pan-India release in five languages, and access to the Gulf — a market worth ₹50–65 Cr that Dhurandhar 2 was simply banned from. Remove those three advantages from Pushpa 2's first week total, and you get roughly ₹500–520 Cr — behind Dhurandhar 2's ₹620 Cr running total.

"D2's ₹65 Cr Monday is the highest non-holiday first Monday in the history of Hindi cinema. Pushpa 2's comparable Monday — also without a holiday — was ₹46 Cr. D2 is ahead by 41%."
Sacnilk box office analysis, March 2026

Where Dhurandhar 2 genuinely leads: The Hindi-language market. D2 is the first Bollywood film to surpass ₹500 Cr net in Hindi in under 6 days. Pushpa 2 took the entire lifetime run of its Hindi version to reach ₹1,123 Cr — and D2 is now on pace to match that figure in its full theatrical run, something that would have seemed impossible before release. In North America, the comparison isn't even close: $14M vs $3.34M. In the UK: £2.2M vs £1.1M. Every Western market broke for Dhurandhar 2.

Where Pushpa 2 leads — and why it matters: The absolute numbers. ₹1,266 Cr vs ₹920 Cr (D0–D14). ₹1,786 Cr vs ₹1,466 Cr. These gaps are real — Pushpa 2 had a four-month theatrical run, a holiday calendar that gifted ₹400+ Cr in Week 1 alone, and multilingual revenue from every Indian circuit including the GCC. Dhurandhar 2 has closed the gap from ₹1,133 Cr behind (at D7) to ₹346 Cr behind WW in just two weeks. That is the story.
