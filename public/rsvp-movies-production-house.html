Analysis Boxoffy Intelligence

Boxoffy · Box Office Intelligence · Updated May 2, 2026

RSVP Movies is one of the most respected mid-tier Hindi production houses. URI: The Surgical Strike has the best ROI in modern Hindi cinema — ₹342 Cr nett against ₹25 Cr budget = 13.7× return. Sam Bahadur (2023) launched Vicky Kaushal as a mainstream lead. Ronnie Screwvala's post-UTV-Disney chapter has been deliberately selective — RSVP releases 2-3 films a year, focused on prestige action or biographical stories. Capital base from upGrad (Screwvala's edtech, valued ~$2.25B at last round) means RSVP never takes bad financing deals. The studio doesn't chase franchise IP but builds reliable mid-budget hits.

Boxoffy Note
RSVP's URI ROI of 13.7× still stands as the best in Hindi cinema. The studio's discipline on budget control + selective output is the template every other mid-tier producer studies.
